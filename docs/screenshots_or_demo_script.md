# Ten-Minute Executive Walkthrough

## 0:00 to 1:00 - Frame

Open the showcase app. Point to the synthetic-data and clinical-use labels. Explain that the suite shows how inpatient flow, ambulatory access, simulation, data quality, and model governance can work together without using real patient data.

## 1:00 to 3:00 - Inpatient Mission Control

Show occupancy, effective beds, ED boarders, discharge confidence, forecast bands, unit pressure heatmap, discharge barriers, and operational options.

## 3:00 to 5:00 - Ambulatory Access

Switch to Ambulatory. Show waitlist size, median wait, third-next-available, no-show risk, backlog by program, template utilization, and access scenario options.

## 5:00 to 7:00 - Simulation Lab

Show how opening surge beds or adding clinic sessions changes projected boarder hours and backlog. Emphasize that outputs are scenario estimates, not directives.

## 7:00 to 8:30 - Methods and Registry

Open Methods. Highlight model cards, validation status, and caveats.

## 8:30 to 10:00 - Snowflake Transferability

Open the Snowsight deployment guide and SQL folder. Explain that the Snowflake version uses curated marts, Snowflake-channel dependencies, and no local CLI requirement.

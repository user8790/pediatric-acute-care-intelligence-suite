# v3 Direct, Derived, and Modelled Classification

The classification system uses synthetic demonstration data and is not validated for clinical decision-making.

## Direct Operational Signals

Direct signals are source-aligned operational facts such as unit census, bed status, ED boarding, referrals, waitlist snapshots, appointment aggregates, diagnostic dependencies, staffing roster aggregates, and public open-data context.

## Derived Operational Intelligence

Derived intelligence is calculated from direct signals, such as occupancy, boarder hours, discharge reliability, effective staffed beds, waitlist pressure, third-next-available, diagnostic readiness, and Gatekeeper health.

## Modelled or Predictive Assets

Modelled outputs include forecasts, risk scores, warning logic, scenario outputs, and clinical-surveillance synthetic signals. They require model cards, governance status, validation evidence, warning caveats, and rollback.

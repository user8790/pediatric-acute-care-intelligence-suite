# Open-Data Source Catalogue

Last checked: 2026-06-10

The implemented connector strategy is cache-first. Open-data fetchers are optional for local refresh and are not used by Streamlit in Snowflake.

| Source | Owner | URL | Intended Use | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FluWatch+ respiratory virus surveillance | Public Health Agency of Canada | https://health-infobase.canada.ca/respiratory-virus-surveillance/ | Weekly respiratory context and surge multipliers | Surveillance context, not hospital demand |
| Alberta respiratory virus dashboard | Government of Alberta | https://www.alberta.ca/stats/dashboard/respiratory-virus-dashboard.htm | Alberta-specific respiratory context | Dashboard values may be revised |
| MSC GeoMet API | Environment and Climate Change Canada | https://api.weather.gc.ca/openapi | Weather context | Ecological context only |
| MSC AQHI | Environment and Climate Change Canada | https://eccc-msc.github.io/open-data/msc-data/aqhi/readme_aqhi_en/ | Air-quality and smoke context | Not patient-level risk |
| Statistics Canada WDS | Statistics Canada | https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/developers | Population denominators by age/geography | Aggregation and geography definitions matter |
| Alberta Open Data | Government of Alberta | https://open.alberta.ca/opendata | Calendar, geography, community context discovery | Dataset-specific licence validation required |

Fallback snapshots are generated by `packages/open_data/fetch_open_data.py` and the synthetic generator.

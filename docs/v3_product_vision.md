# v3 Product Vision

This v3 pass frames the suite as a Provincial Pediatric Acute Care Intelligence Operating Layer.

It remains synthetic demonstration data and is not validated for clinical decision-making.

## Product Intent

The showcase augments Connect Care-era operations rather than replacing or writing back to Connect Care. The future real-data path is read-only curated Snowflake views for operational signals, plus Snowflake writeback tables for operational intelligence learning memory.

## Assumptions

- No direct identifiers are generated or displayed.
- Public showcase pages should not include the internal Walkthrough tab.
- Vercel showcase ambition can move faster than the Streamlit/Snowsight path.
- Streamlit remains practical for Snowflake Anaconda baseline packages. Advanced packages are optional research adapters, not core Snowsight dependencies.
- Every major output should show direct, derived, or modelled classification plus readiness, freshness, confidence, caveat, and lineage.

## Product Areas

- Today pediatric system posture.
- Inpatient intelligence.
- Ambulatory access intelligence.
- Clinical surveillance and predictive asset layer.
- Scenario simulation lab.
- AHA Gatekeeper control plane.
- Learning system memory.
- Future real-data wiring.
